# Public Research Scope

This repository is a public-facing description of an active road-crack segmentation research project.

## What is public

The public repository is limited to:

- the research problem and motivation;
- high-level experimental directions;
- the status of major workstreams;
- the evaluation criteria used to assess segmentation quality;
- a record that the implementation exists but is intentionally withheld.

## What remains private

The following are kept outside the public repository while the study is being developed:

- model source code;
- custom loss implementations;
- training and evaluation pipelines;
- notebooks and patching utilities;
- exact experiment configurations;
- checkpoints and model weights;
- raw logs and intermediate result files;
- unpublished implementation details that may form part of a future manuscript.

## Why the code is withheld

The project is still under active investigation and the team is considering a journal-oriented publication path. Keeping the implementation private avoids publishing incomplete mechanisms or experimental details before the study has been finalized.

The absence of code in this public repository therefore reflects the current disclosure policy of the project, not the absence of an implementation.

## Current interpretation of results

Historical experiments are treated as internal evidence rather than final benchmark claims. Comparisons are being consolidated across architectures and training objectives before any headline result is presented publicly.

This policy is intended to keep the public repository useful as a portfolio and project summary without exposing material that may still change or become part of a manuscript.
